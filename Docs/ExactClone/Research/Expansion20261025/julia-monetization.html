Pixel Flow by Loom Games is making over $500K a day!

Journal Julia Iljuk February 24

Julia Iljuk

13 Posts

𝗣𝗶𝘅𝗲𝗹 𝗙𝗹𝗼𝘄 𝗯𝘆 Loom Games, $𝟱𝟬𝟬𝗞+ 𝗮 𝗱𝗮𝘆. 𝗛𝗲𝗿𝗲 𝗶𝘀 𝘁𝗵𝗲𝗶𝗿 𝗲𝗮𝗿𝗹𝘆 𝗺𝗼𝗻𝗲𝘁𝗶𝘀𝗮𝘁𝗶𝗼𝗻 𝗽𝗹𝗮𝘆𝗯𝗼𝗼𝗸.

Just think about these numbers: half a million $$ a day!
That’s a ~$180M yearly.

Not because of some revolutionary monetisation system. Because of a simple one that does its job well.

I’ve played the game, and here are the key takeaways.

𝘖𝘯𝘣𝘰𝘢𝘳𝘥𝘪𝘯𝘨:

✅ No endless loading screens or long tutorials. Level 1 does the onboarding in seconds. Instant fun.
✅ The first event (Lava Quest) is shown early on, but locked until level 70. It gives a future promise and motivation to continue.

𝘔𝘰𝘯𝘦𝘵𝘪𝘴𝘢𝘵𝘪𝘰𝘯:

✅ Level 10: soft monetisation kicks in as a double coin reward for watching a rewarded ad (pic 1).
✅ After a failed attempt: you can spend in-game currency or buy a Fail Offer for $6.99 to avoid losing a life. Classic loss aversion (pic 2).
✅ Level 20: forced ads appear. And immediately after that – a No Ads Offer to remove forced ads (pic 3).
✅ Further on, forced ads are shown after each level. 𝙄𝙣 𝙩𝙝𝙞𝙨 𝙨𝙚𝙩𝙪𝙥, 𝙖𝙙𝙨 𝙙𝙤𝙣’𝙩 𝙟𝙪𝙨𝙩 𝙢𝙤𝙣𝙚𝙩𝙞𝙨𝙚 – 𝙩𝙝𝙚𝙮 𝙖𝙘𝙩𝙞𝙫𝙚𝙡𝙮 𝙧𝙚𝙞𝙣𝙛𝙤𝙧𝙘𝙚 𝙄𝘼𝙋 𝙙𝙚𝙢𝙖𝙣𝙙 (𝙉𝙤 𝘼𝙙𝙨 𝙗𝙪𝙣𝙙𝙡𝙚).
✅ Level 27: another full-screen No Ads Offer push. 
✅ Level 36: Pixel Pass is introduced (pic 4).
✅ The shop: clean and simple. A few gold packs, a few bundles, and two clear heroes – the No Ads bundle at $8.99 and Pixel Pass at $9.99. I’d bet these two drive the bulk of revenue. (pic 5)

Still playing and learning.
But this one is a masterclass in doing less, better.
